## [2026-06-18] concept | level bias vs dynamics
Added entry to `concept-map.md` between "naive baseline" and identifiability concepts. Explains the level/dynamics decomposition of a prediction error and why a model can lose to a trivial baseline purely on level bias while its dynamics are fine — exactly the failure mode the 2026-06-16 ambient-reference bug caused. **Gotcha**: always include a constant baseline that already has the correct level (T_inf for us); otherwise you cannot tell whether your model is winning because of better dynamics or just having the right asymptote. The level-correct baseline isolates the dynamics contribution.

## [2026-10-02] ingest | Lin et al. 2012 — Quadruple adaptive observer of core temperature
Filed `sources/lin-2012-adaptive-observer.md`. ACC 2012, pp. 578–583. RLS on a Laplace-domain parametric model identifies `C_c`, `C_s`, `R_e` and `R_c` separately, in noise-free simulation, with `R_u(V)` known from coolant velocity. Table I implies a split of 0.934. Temperature-dependent `R_e` biases `C_c`, `C_s` and `R_c` and corrupts `T_c` while `T_s` stays fit (Figs. 6–7). One correction to the brief from verification: the regressor multiplying `µ = 1/C_s` is `s·(T_f − T_s)/R_u(V)`, not `(T_f − T_s)/R_u(V)`. The `(T_f − T_s)/R_u(V)` regressor belongs to `β`.

## [2026-10-02] ingest | Richardson & Howey 2015 — Sensorless temperature estimation via impedance
Filed `sources/richardson-howey-2015-sensorless.md`. 1-D radial conduction with uniform `ρc_p` (polynomial approximation). The EKF measures 215 Hz impedance; a dual EKF estimates `h`. `k_t`, `c_p` and `h` are fitted offline against measured core and surface temperatures on an A123 26650 LFP cell.
